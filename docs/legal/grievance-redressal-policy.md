## 3. How to File a Grievance
Users, parents, legal guardians, or data principals may submit a formal complaint or inquiry regarding:
1. Minor data protection and parental consent revocations.
2. Inappropriate or abusive user conduct on the platform.
3. Content takedown and copyright infringement notices.
4. DPDP data rights requests (Access, Correction, Erasure, Grievance escalation).

### Information to Include in Your Grievance:
- Full Name and Registered Email Address associated with the account.
- Nature of the grievance and relevant URLs/User IDs.
- Supporting documents or evidence (e.g. screenshots, email headers).
- Clear statement of the relief or remediation sought.

## 4. Timelines for Grievance Resolution
- **Acknowledgement:** Within **twenty-four (24) hours** of receipt.
- **Investigation & Resolution:** Within **fifteen (15) working days** from the date of receipt.
- **Immediate Takedown (Minor Safety / Non-consensual imagery):** Processed within **twenty-four (24) hours** of verified notice.
