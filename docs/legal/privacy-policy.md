## 1. Overview & Commitment to Privacy
Edworld Co ("we", "us", "our") operates a personalized career readiness platform. We are deeply committed to protecting the privacy and personal data of all users, with heightened protections and strict compliance with India's **Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023** and global youth privacy frameworks.

---

## 2. Information We Collect
1. **Account Information:** Name, email address, password hash, and explicit Date of Birth (DOB). *Note: Google OAuth sign-in only provides name and email; DOB is actively and explicitly collected within our own onboarding flow.*
2. **Profile & Skill Data:** Self-reported interests, calibrated effective levels (0, 1, 2), primary career goals, and resume contents.
3. **Audio & Video Data:** Audio responses recorded during Mock Interview practice sessions (processed for real-time speech evaluation and transcription).
4. **Minor Safety & Guardian Information:** Parent/guardian full name, email address, and relationship for learners under 18 years of age.
5. **System & Audit Logs:** Server-side logs capturing action type, actor ID, target entity, and timestamp stored in an immutable `auditLogs` ledger for security and accountability.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Data
- **Skill Personalization:** Calibrating question difficulty, computing Job Description (JD) match scores, and recommending daily practice tasks.
- **Minor Protection & Consent:** Verifying parental authorization and preventing minors from accessing adult networking or open job boards.
- **Security & Incident Response:** Maintaining server-side audit logs to investigate security events, policy violations, and grievance filings.
- **Service Delivery:** Rendering ATS-compatible resumes and generating PDF exports.

---

## 4. Minors' Data Protection (DPDP Act Compliance)
- **Zero Behavioral Advertising:** We never track or serve targeted behavioral advertisements to minor users.
- **No Public Minor Profiles:** Profiles of users in the Junior Track (13–15) and Explorer Track (16–18) are permanently private and unreachable by public search.
- **No Direct Messaging:** Minor learners cannot access open peer messaging or adult networking directories.
- **Verified Guardian Consent:** Guardian consent is verified via secure email confirmation tokens prior to persistent profile activation.

---

## 5. Data Retention & Erasure Policy
- **Active Data:** Retained as long as your account remains active.
- **Audit Logs:** Retained for a rolling period of **730 days (2 years)** for compliance, security auditing, and grievance investigation, after which they are automatically pruned by scheduled maintenance functions.
- **Data Rights:** Users may request a full export of their personal data or request permanent account erasure at any time through our DPDP Data Rights portal.

---

## 6. Grievance Redressal Officer
In compliance with the DPDP Act and Indian Information Technology Rules:
- **Grievance Officer:** Legal & Data Protection Office, Edworld Co
- **Email:** `grievance@edworld.co`
- **Response Time:** Acknowledgement within 24 hours; resolution within 15 working days.
